# Titan Training Pro

Create a fully functional, professional fitness mobile and web app called "Titan Training". 



The app should be a complete fitness ecosystem including:

- Gym workouts (muscle-building with equipment)

- Calisthenics workouts (bodyweight training)

- Personalized diet plans

- Exercise demonstration videos

- User progress tracking

- AI-powered workout recommendations

- Premium monetization system



Target Audience:

Men and women, ages 16–45, looking to lose fat, gain muscle, or improve overall fitness.



Design & UI:

- Dark theme with black (#0B0B0B), dark gray (#121212), red (#E50914), orange (#FF6A00), yellow (#FFC107)

- Modern, sleek, tech-inspired design

- Rounded cards and buttons with subtle neon highlights

- Intuitive navigation for all age groups

- High-resolution visuals and animations



User Onboarding:

- Ask for gender (male/female)

- Age

- Height

- Weight

- Training level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)

- Goal (fat loss, hypertrophy, strength, conditioning, transformation)

- Body type (ectomorph, mesomorph, endomorph)



Automatic Adjustments:

- Adjust workout intensity and progression based on body type and fitness goal

- Adjust diet plan based on body type, gender, weight, activity level, and goal



Workout System:



1) Gym Workouts:

- Organized by muscle groups: Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs, Glutes, Abs

- Include sets, reps, rest times, progression plan

- Each exercise must have: HD video, step-by-step instructions, muscles worked, common mistakes, difficulty level



2) Calisthenics Workouts:

- Include bodyweight exercises: Push-ups, Pull-ups, Dips, Handstand, Muscle-ups, L-sit, Pistol squat, Dragon flag

- Each exercise with HD instructional video and step-by-step guide



Exercise Database:

- Include ~300 exercises covering Gym and Calisthenics

- Each exercise includes: Name, Muscle group, Equipment, Level, Description, Video prompt

- Video prompts for AI generation:

  Gym Exercise Prompt: 

    "Create a high-quality 10–20 second HD fitness tutorial video demonstrating [exercise name] in a modern gym, showing full body movement, perfect posture, muscle engagement, multiple camera angles, loopable, professional fitness tutorial style."

  Calisthenics Exercise Prompt:

    "Create a 10–20 second HD calisthenics tutorial showing [exercise name] in an outdoor park or minimalist gym, full body visible, controlled movement, perfect technique, loopable video."



Diet & Nutrition System:

- Personalized diet plans based on user goal, body type, gender, weight, and activity level

- Macronutrient breakdowns for protein, carbs, and fats

- Food examples: Chicken, eggs, fish, rice, potatoes, oats, vegetables, healthy fats

- Plans for: Hypertrophy, Fat Loss, Definition



Progress Tracking:

- Track weight, body fat %, muscle mass, measurements

- Visual charts and graphs

- Workout history and streaks

- Goal achievement notifications



AI Recommendation Engine:

- Adjusts workouts and difficulty automatically based on user performance and progress

- Suggests new exercises and increased load when ready

- Alerts for stagnation and recovery periods



Monetization:

- Freemium model:

  Free: basic workouts, limited videos, basic diet plans, basic progress tracking

  Premium: full access to workouts, diet plans, exercise library, HD videos, transformation programs

- Suggested price: R$19.90/month or R$97/year

- Optional in-app purchases for specialized programs or ebooks



Interface Screens:

- Home: Daily workout summary, progress overview, start button

- Workout: List of exercises with sets, reps, and timer

- Exercise: Video demo, instructions, muscles worked, tips

- Nutrition: Daily meal plan with calories and macros

- Progress: Charts and analytics

- Profile: Personal info, goals, stats, history

- Settings: App theme, notifications, account settings



Extra Features:

- Rest timer and countdown

- Series/reps counter

- Workout reminders

- Offline mode for workouts

- Searchable exercise library

- Push notifications for motivation

- Achievement badges



Logo Prompt:

"Create a minimalist but aggressive vector logo for Titan Training. Include Spartan helmet or titan silhouette. Colors: black background, red, orange, yellow highlights. Bold athletic font. Style: modern, powerful, professional fitness brand."



Outcome:

The app should be a complete, ready-to-launch fitness application that combines **muscle-building, calisthenics, personalized nutrition, AI recommendations, and premium monetization**. 

All exercises should have **AI-generated HD video prompts** for demonstration. The app must feel **motivating, modern, and professional**, like top-tier apps such as Nike Training Club, Freeletics, and Fitbod.



Make the app fully interactive, modular, and ready to expand with new exercises, diet plans, and AI capabilities.

Create a powerful modern fitness logo for an app called Titan Training.
